About Us

Far Away From a Typical Recruitment Agency

Over the last few years, we have firmly established ourselves as an emerging skilled manpower consulting firm in India and the Middle East. We started in 2012, consulting and supplying manpower to major firms in India.

We are a multidisciplinary manpower consulting firm specialising in all types of front-end and back-end manpower supply. Our extraordinary client servicing and the quality of talent we provide became the basis of our rapid expansion into the Middle East and North African markets.

Apna Travels handles permanent, fixed-term and temporary recruitment, from entry-level positions to managerial levels, for SMEs and large corporations working at domestic, national, international and multinational levels.

Message from the Board

It is a great pleasure for us to introduce ourselves as an emerging and trustworthy manpower consultant firm authorised to supply skilled manpower in India and other foreign countries.

We believe human potential should be utilised to the maximum to make the world a better place. We specialise in providing skilled, qualified, innovative and proactive manpower that helps your organisation stand apart from the rest of the industry.
